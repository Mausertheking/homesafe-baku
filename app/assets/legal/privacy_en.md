# Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

## Who we are

HomeSafe is operated by [operator name and address] ("we"). Questions about your data: [contact e-mail].

## What we collect and why

- **Account:** your email address and password. Google Firebase stores the password only in encrypted form. We do not collect phone numbers.
- **Settings:** your language, and whether you rent or own your home.
- **Home profile:** district, building age, type of heating, where your gas water heater is, whether you have a CO alarm, and whether you have a written rental contract. We do not ask for your street address here.
- **Check answers and results**, including symptoms such as headache or dizziness. These stay on your phone unless you tap "Save to evidence log".
- **Evidence log:** photos, notes, symptom notes, check results and letters you save, with the time our server recorded them and a digital fingerprint (SHA-256).
- **Letters:** the name, address and details you type into a letter.
- **AI assistant:** your messages and any photo you attach.
- **Usage statistics** without personal details (for example "a check was completed"), to improve the app.

You can use the safety checks without an account. Then nothing except anonymous usage statistics leaves your phone.

## Health information

Answers about symptoms can be health information, which the law protects specially. We use them only to give you safety guidance, and we store them on our servers only if you choose to save them.

## How we use it

- To run the app: checks, evidence log, reports, letters and reminders.
- **AI assistant:** your message is sent to Google's Gemini service together with your home profile and latest check result. Your name, email and address are never sent. We do not store the conversation. Messages about emergencies are never sent to the AI.
- To prevent abuse, we record when the assistant is used and the length of each message, but never the message itself.
- We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

## Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud). The servers may be outside Azerbaijan, for example in the European Union. Only you can read your data; our security rules prevent other users from accessing it, and photos are never public.

## How long we keep it

- Your account and saved data: until you delete your account.
- Items you remove from the evidence log are hidden, but kept until you delete your account, so that the record stays reliable.
- Report, letter and export download links work for 15 minutes; the files are deleted after one day.
- The daily message counter for the assistant is deleted after three days.

## Your rights

Under the Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan "On Personal Data" (No. 998-IIIQ of 11 May 2010) you may ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected, and withdraw your consent.

- **Settings → Export all my data** gives you a file with everything we hold, including your photos.
- **Settings → Delete my account and data** permanently deletes your account and all your data at once.

For anything else, write to [contact e-mail].
